Started as a birthday party favor

Miss Candy started when a kitchen-table batch of lollipops for a birthday party got passed around the neighborhood — and then requested again the next month, and the month after that.

We still cook everything the same way: small batches, real ingredients, and a taste test before anything gets packaged. If a batch doesn't taste right, it doesn't ship.

How it's made

Three steps, every batch

Nothing here is mass-produced — every step still happens by hand.

i.

Cooked in small batches

Sugar work is done in pots small enough to watch closely — no industrial cookers, no shortcuts on timing.

ii.

Poured & shaped by hand

Lollipops are poured into molds one at a time; gummies are cut and dusted the same way.

iii.

Tasted before it ships

Every batch gets tasted by someone in the kitchen before it's packaged — no exceptions.
